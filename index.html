Content automation

Your newsroom's feed,
on autopilot.

SMAS watches the RSS feeds your team already publishes to, turns each new article into a platform-ready post, and publishes it to the Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok pages you've connected — no copy-pasting, no missed news cycle.

01

Fetch

Polls each connected RSS source on a schedule and detects new articles the moment they're published.

02

Rewrite

Drafts a title, caption, and hashtags in Azerbaijani, then renders the article art onto your page's template.

03

Publish

Posts to every connected account for that source, or waits for a human nod first — your call, per source.

Built for operators, not developers

SMAS is run through a Telegram bot: connect a Facebook Page or Instagram Business account once via OAuth, add the RSS feeds you want mirrored, and the pipeline above runs on its own from that point forward. Approve posts by hand when you want a second look, or let fully-automated sources publish the moment an article lands.

Every account you connect stays yours — SMAS holds only the access you grant, publishes only what its rules generate from your own feeds, and can be disconnected from the same bot at any time.
