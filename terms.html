01Acceptance of these terms

These terms apply to anyone who connects a Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, or TikTok account to SMAS ("the service"), whether directly or as an operator managing pages on behalf of a publisher. Connecting an account, or using the Telegram bot that drives SMAS, means you accept the terms below.

02What SMAS does

SMAS reads RSS feeds you configure, drafts a title, caption, and hashtag set for each new article, renders it onto your page's visual template, and publishes the result to the accounts you've connected — either automatically or after your explicit approval, depending on how you've configured that source. You can also compose and publish one-off posts directly through the Telegram bot.

03Connected accounts & authorization

Connecting an account is done through each platform's own OAuth flow (Meta Login, TikTok Login Kit). You must be an administrator, editor, or otherwise authorized manager of any Page or account you connect — SMAS will only ever publish through the specific permission grant that platform's login screen shows you before you approve it.

You can revoke this access at any time, either from inside the Telegram bot or from the connected-apps settings of the platform itself. Revoking access stops all future publishing to that account immediately.

04Acceptable use

  • Only connect Pages and accounts you're authorized to manage.
  • Don't use SMAS to publish spam, deceptive content, or material that violates Meta's Platform Terms or TikTok's Developer Terms and Community Guidelines.
  • Don't use the automated-rewrite pipeline to strip attribution from source articles in a way that misleads readers about where the content originated.
  • Don't attempt to exceed, circumvent, or abuse the rate limits either platform's API imposes.

05Content responsibility

You're responsible for the accuracy and legality of everything published through your connected accounts, including text that SMAS's writing assistant drafted from your source feed. The assistant is a drafting tool, not an editor of record — review automated output before enabling unattended publishing for a source, if accuracy matters for that source.

06Service availability

SMAS is offered on a best-effort basis, without an uptime guarantee. Feed polling, rendering, or publishing may pause during maintenance or upstream outages at Meta, TikTok, or the RSS sources themselves.
