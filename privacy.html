01Overview

SMAS is a publishing pipeline, not an audience-data product: it moves content from your RSS feeds to your own connected Facebook, Instagram, and (soon) TikTok accounts. This policy covers what we collect to make that happen, and nothing beyond it — we don't read your accounts' follower data, direct messages, or analytics beyond the follower and view counts you can already see on your own dashboard.

02What we collect

DataWhy
Telegram user & chat IDIdentify you as an operator inside the bot; route messages and approvals.
Platform account ID + access tokenGranted via Meta Login / TikTok Login Kit; used only to publish to that specific account. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
RSS feed URLs you addPolled on a schedule to detect new articles for that source.
Article text, images, and links you publish or approveRewritten, rendered, and posted to your connected accounts.
Follower count & view snapshotsShown back to you in the operator dashboard; not shared outside your own view of it.

03How we use it

Every field above exists to run the fetch → rewrite → publish pipeline described in the Terms of Service, or to show you your own connected-account statistics. We don't sell data, run advertising off it, or share it with anyone outside the third parties in the next section that are technically required to publish on your behalf.

04Third parties

  • Meta Graph API — publishes to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts, under Meta's own Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok Content Posting API — publishes to connected TikTok accounts, under TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI — drafts captions and hashtags from your source article text; article content is sent for this purpose only, not your access tokens or Telegram identifiers.

Each of these only receives the specific data required for the step it performs.

05Storage & security

Access tokens are encrypted before storage and decrypted only at the moment a post is published. The database and bot run on infrastructure the operator controls directly — there is no third-party hosting of your data beyond the API calls in the section above.

06Retention

Connected-account data is kept for as long as the connection stays active. Disconnecting an account (from the bot or from the platform's own app-permissions screen) stops publishing immediately; the associated access token is deleted on the next cleanup pass.

07Your rights

You can disconnect any account at any time through the Telegram bot. To request full deletion of your operator record and any retained history, email us — see contact below.

08Children's privacy

SMAS is a publishing tool for media operators and isn't directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 13.

09Changes to this policy

We'll update the date above whenever this policy changes. Material changes — a new category of data, a new third party — will also be announced through the Telegram bot.
